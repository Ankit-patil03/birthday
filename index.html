My Kitty 🎂

Happy birthday, my love. Wherever you're reading this from, I hope you know just how much you mean to me.

Thank you for sixteen months of choosing us. For choosing us through the distance, the different time zones, the late-night calls, the sleepy goodnights, and all those moments when all we wanted was to be beside each other but had to settle for a screen.

Somehow, you made the distance feel smaller. Every time I hear your voice, the miles between us disappear for a little while. Every call feels like having you right next to me, and every "I miss you" is just another reminder of how much having you in my life means to me.

There are days when I wish I could forget about the distance completely when I wish I could just show up at your door, hold you, look into your eyes, and tell you everything I normally have to fit into a message or a call.

And especially today, I wish I could be there. I wish I could be the first person you see, hold you a little longer than usual, kiss your forehead, and watch you smile while I tell you happy birthday in person.

But today, I don't want to think about how far away you are. I want to think about how incredibly lucky I am that, out of everyone in this world, it's you on the other side of my phone. It's you I wait for. It's you I miss. It's you I want to tell everything to. And it's you I keep choosing.

So here's to every picture you send me that I stare at longer than I'll admit, every call that lasts way longer than we planned, every stupid little conversation that somehow becomes one of my favorite parts of the day, and every "I miss you" that I will never get tired of hearing.

And here's to the day when we won't have to say "I miss you" through a phone anymore. When birthdays won't be celebrated from different places, goodnight won't mean ending a call, and I'll finally be able to reach for you instead of reaching for my phone.

Until then, just remember this: distance may keep me from holding you today, but it has never once stopped me from loving you.

Happy birthday, my Kitty. Te amo today, and every day I've been counting since.
